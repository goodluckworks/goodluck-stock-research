# v2.0 Production Research System

`stock-research-v2.0.0` is a production research system for public equity research.

## Stable Capabilities

- Controlled live SEC ingestion.
- Filing identity and amendment discovery.
- Scoped production XBRL parser MVP for selected 10-K / 10-Q fields.
- Normalized financial statement generation with provenance.
- Data quality and reconciliation gates.
- Valuation and scenario analysis.
- Evidence-backed research conclusion engine.
- Approval-gated production research report packets.
- MRVL production research pilot.
- Full audit trail and red-team checks.

## Production Boundaries

- No broker integration.
- No trading execution.
- No order routing.
- No credential or secret workflow.
- No personalized financial advice.
- No position sizing.
- No trade instructions.
- Research conclusions are evidence-backed and approval-gated.
