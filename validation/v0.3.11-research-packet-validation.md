# v0.3.11 Research Packet Validation

## 1. Scope

v0.3.11 adds a sandbox/review-only research packet builder. Packets are readable summaries of pipeline outputs, not final investment reports.

## 2. Files Added

- `goodluck-stock-research/pipeline/research_packet_builder.py`
- `goodluck-stock-research/pipeline/tests/run_research_packet_tests.py`
- `goodluck-stock-research/schemas/research_packet_manifest_schema.json`
- `goodluck-stock-research/examples/research_packets/`

## 3. Packet Types

| Packet | Result | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| EXMP full sandbox packet | Pass | Includes pipeline summary, populated fields, omitted/unresolved fields, ratio dry-run summary and audit references |
| EXMP partial sandbox packet | Pass | Includes partial-field caveats and unavailable ratios |
| MRVL review-only packet | Pass | No approval, no promotion, no normalized CSV, no v0.2.x dry-run |
| NVDA review-only packet | Pass | No approval, no promotion, no normalized CSV, no v0.2.x dry-run |
| AVGO review-only packet | Pass | No approval, no promotion, no normalized CSV, no v0.2.x dry-run |
