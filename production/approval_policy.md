# Production Approval Policy

## Publication States

- `draft_unreviewed`: system-generated, not reviewed.
- `reviewed_internal`: reviewed but not approved for publication.
- `approved_internal`: approved for internal research use.
- `publishable_research_packet`: human-approved and audit-cleared.
- `blocked`: cannot be used until issues are resolved.

## Publishable Requirements

- Quality gate pass.
- Conclusion audit pass.
- Report audit pass.
- No forbidden fields.
- Human approval record.
- Full audit trail.
