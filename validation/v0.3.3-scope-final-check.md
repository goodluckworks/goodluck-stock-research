## 4. Extraction Scope Design Summary

The design limits a future prototype to nine consolidated GAAP fields:

- Revenue.
- Gross profit.
- Operating income.
- Net income.
- Operating cash flow.
- Capital expenditure.
- Cash and equivalents.
- Total debt.
- Diluted weighted-average shares.

The design defines:

- Versioned standard/fallback tag mappings.
- Company extension-tag review requirements.
- Entity, context, period, unit, sign and scale rules.
- Consolidated-versus-dimensional filtering.
- Amendment/restatement lineage.
- Field-specific reconciliation, including capex sign and debt
  double-counting controls.
- `draft_extracted_unreviewed` candidate status.
- Mandatory human `approve/adjust` gate and separate promotion step.
- Structured unresolved/failure behavior.

No parser, extractor, tag resolver, normalized writer or promotion command was
implemented.
