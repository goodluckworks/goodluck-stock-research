# Real-Filing-Excerpt Review Drills

These files are short, license-safe offline review drills. They are heavily reduced, artificial
excerpt structures inspired by public filing layouts, not complete filings and not authoritative
financial data.

Rules:

- Excerpt only.
- Not a complete filing.
- Not live extraction.
- Not company-approved or SEC-authoritative data.
- Drill only; no approved values.
- MRVL, NVDA and AVGO outputs must remain draft-only and unpromoted.

The fixtures are designed to exercise reviewer ergonomics, taxonomy relationship metadata,
checklist prompts and quality scoring.
