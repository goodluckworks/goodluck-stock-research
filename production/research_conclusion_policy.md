# Research Conclusion Policy

Allowed research views:

- `constructive`
- `neutral`
- `cautious`
- `negative`
- `insufficient_evidence`

Allowed content:

- Evidence-backed thesis.
- Valuation scenario.
- Confidence score.
- Key upside and downside drivers.
- Data quality caveats.

Forbidden content:

- Personalized financial advice.
- Trade instruction.
- Position sizing.
- Guaranteed return.
- Broker order.
- Auto-buy or auto-sell instruction.
