## 7. Remaining Limitations

- Company, CIK, accession, form and filing URL remain user-supplied.
- The adapter does not discover amendments or independently determine the
  latest effective filing.
- The adapter does not parse XBRL or extract financial values.
- Manual normalization remains required before v0.2.x calculation use.
- A real SEC live smoke was not run by default; fixture and mocked-live paths
  are the release acceptance basis.
