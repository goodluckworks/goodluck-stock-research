## 4. Provenance Coverage Review

Required source fields are covered:

- `source_id`, `source_name`, `source_url`, `source_type`
- `retrieved_at`, `source_date`, `filing_date`
- `reporting_period`, `fiscal_period`, `fiscal_year`, `fiscal_quarter`
- `currency`, `amount_unit`, `per_share_unit`, `market_session`
- `source_status`, `data_status`, `confidence`
- `field_provenance`, `transformation_notes`

Field-level records include original field names, locators, source IDs, formula/transformation, units, confidence and notes. Missing values are explicit unresolved records rather than zero or blank.

Source hierarchy covers official filings, company IR, earnings releases, transcripts, presentations, vendors, secondary pages, user-provided data and analyst assumptions.

## 5. Freshness / Cache Review

The design covers:

- Intraday, daily market snapshot, filing, quarter, annual, transcript, profile and assumption freshness classes.
- Timestamp, timezone, market session and delay attributes.
- Stale-but-historical versus stale-blocking behavior.
- Immutable raw snapshots and no silent overwrite.
- Versioned normalized inputs and derived outputs.
- Restatement/supersession lineage and dependent-output invalidation.
- Metadata-only retention for restricted evidence.
- Cache fallback without pretending stale evidence is fresh.

## 6. Failure Mode Coverage Review

All required failure modes are specified:

- Ticker and company/CIK ambiguity.
- Missing filing and restatement.
- Unit, currency and split/share-count mismatch.
- Stale market snapshot.
- Source/vendor conflicts.
- Segment reporting and acquisition/divestiture breaks.
- Missing normalized reconciliation.
- Future network, authentication and rate-limit behavior.
- Unsupported company types.

Blocking failures stop affected normalization. Recoverable failures remain visible in warnings/unresolved fields. No path permits fabricated fallback.

## 7. Schema Contract Review

- Adapter metadata freezes type, version, mode, key requirement, allowlist and prohibited actions.
- Input contract captures identity, fiscal period, request type, source preference and as-of date.
- Output contract is an allowlist of manifests, provenance, normalized paths, warnings, errors and unresolved fields.
- Error contract is structured and machine-readable.
- Source manifest separates source reliability (`source_status`) from data availability/freshness (`data_status`).
- Raw snapshot schema requires immutable local evidence, checksum and supersession.
- Field provenance requires lineage for every populated normalized field.

The schemas prevent adapter output from directly carrying target price, research status, recommendation, position size or portfolio action.

## 9. Real Case Coverage

### MRVL

- Supports GAAP special-item warnings and missing normalized reconciliation.
- Preserves AI-related/data-center/design-win distinctions.
- Retains `OCF - capex` as an FCFE research approximation.

### NVDA

- Supports cash plus marketable securities with liquidity notes.
- Requires split-adjusted share-count provenance.
- Distinguishes Data Center revenue from unreported AI-only revenue.
- Handles GAAP equity-security gains without inventing normalized earnings.

### AVGO

- Supports VMware acquisition comparability break.
- Preserves goodwill, intangibles, amortization, restructuring and debt evidence.
- Separates semiconductor and infrastructure-software source facts.
- Treats peer grouping and normalized reconciliation as explicit judgments.

## 12. Implementation Readiness

- Ready for v0.3 implementation: No, pending explicit user approval and the decisions below.
- Recommended first adapter: Narrow no-key `official_filing_adapter` for a user-specified CIK/form/accession that writes immutable raw evidence plus manifests and normalized local inputs.
- Required user decision before implementation:
  - Exact first-adapter scope and official source.
  - Raw evidence retention location and retention period.
  - Whether implementation may perform network access in a separate approved phase.
  - Identity resolution behavior and supported filing types.
  - Whether any future optional-key adapter is in scope; default recommendation is no.
- Risks:
  - Filing taxonomy and XBRL mapping variability.
  - Restatements and entity/share-class ambiguity.
  - Licensing restrictions for transcripts and market data.
  - Future implementations weakening provenance or silently filling missing fields.

Design review result: **Pass**. The architecture is ready for user review, not automatic implementation.
